## Lessons carried from v1 (locked rules for this channel)

- Every spoken line = **subject + verb + object.** Fragments live only in captions/stamps.
- The voice model reads exactly what's written; it cannot add connective tissue.
- A written hook that scores 6/6 can still die as audio — Gate 4 exists to catch it.

## Predicted weak spots + mitigations

1. **Ladder length 7.3–26.6 (~19 s of "bigger, bigger, bigger").**
   Mitigations: verb chant with variation (grows taller / climbs into / passes / clears /
   grows bigger), every stamp escalates the object class (building → mountain → planet
   boundary → Moon → star → probe → universe), the Everest stamp is a silent read-it-
   yourself receipt, the FOLDS-LEFT ticker runs a second motion plane, whips at 13.2/12.3-style
   boundaries, and the only spoken fold count ("one hundred") lands as a scale.
2. **The quiet breather (5.3–7.3)** — the post-hook dip is real, and the voice goes quiet
   for 1.8 s here on purpose. Mitigation: the picture is busy (sheet folds itself ×7 with
   tick sounds, cards deny then accepted), so sound-off and sound-on both get motion.
   If the 3 s retention graph dips here, A/B the bridge line shorter ("Here's what happens.").

3. **Reality check downshift (32.7–38.6)** — the low-energy valley after the universe jump.
   Mitigation: 5.9 s max, one hard cut, zero lore — it is the trust beat that makes the
   final number legal. Its quiet is the contrast the payoff explodes against.
4. **Post-payoff drift** — the "video's over" shrug.
   Mitigation: echo is the literal frame-1 replica + a one-sentence mantra + cut to black;
   no captions past the payoff; the brand QC seal prints on the black tail only.
5. **~55 s runtime** — longer than the 35 s default, inside the 60 s cap.
   Justified: the ladder IS the content; every shot escalates; loops chain with no dead
   stretch; 115 spoken units at fenrir's measured ~0.45 s/unit is the cost of full
   sentences (v1 lesson — no telegraphic filler allowed).
